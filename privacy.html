Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

Bidness ("we", "our", or "us") is operated by James Boyer, Waterloo, Ontario, Canada. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use the Bidness iOS app.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: name and email address when you create an account.
  • Listing content: photos, titles, descriptions, and prices you submit for auctions. Photos are checked automatically for nudity and violence, and listing text for items we don't allow.
  • City selection: the city you choose for your listings. We do not collect GPS or precise location data.
  • Purchase records: subscription and in-app purchase receipts, processed by Apple and RevenueCat. We do not store payment card details.
  • Marketplace activity: your bids, the questions and answers you post on listings, ratings and the notes you leave with them, pickup bookings and check-ins, and the reports and appeals you send. We keep these to run auctions, settle disputes, and prevent fraud.
  • Payment details for a sale: if you sell an item, the e-transfer email address or phone number you enter is shared only with your winning buyer. Bidness never handles the money between buyers and sellers.
  • Device information: a push notification token so we can send you alerts, and an Apple App Attest check that confirms requests come from the real Bidness app. We do not use advertising identifiers or track you across other apps or websites.
  • Communications: emails you send us.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To operate and provide the Bidness marketplace.
  • To process bids, sales, and in-app purchases.
  • To send transactional notifications (bid updates, auction results, questions on your listings, pickup reminders).
  • To improve app features and fix bugs.
  • To detect and prevent fraud or policy violations.

3. Third-Party Services

Bidness uses the following third-party services, each governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Firebase (Google): sign-in, database, photo storage, cloud functions, push notification delivery, and App Check.
  • Google Cloud Vision: automatic checks of listing photos.
  • RevenueCat: subscription management and purchase validation.
  • Apple: in-app purchases and TestFlight distribution.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with the third-party services listed above as necessary to operate the app, or when required by law.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

We keep your account and listing data while your account is active. You can delete your account at any time in the app: Profile → Settings → Delete my data. (If you're in the middle of a sale or have a winning bid, finish or cancel it first.)

Deleting your account removes your profile, your listings and their photos, and your follows, saved items, and notifications. A few records stay because other people rely on them: a listing you sold stays as the buyer's record of their purchase (without your payment details), and the bids and ratings you left on other people's deals remain part of their history. If your account is suspended when you delete it, we keep your email address for 30 days so the suspension can't be undone by signing up again. You can also ask us to delete your data by email.

6. Age Requirement

Bidness is only for people 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18.

7. Your Rights (PIPEDA – Canada)

As a Canadian user, you have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal information. To exercise these rights, contact us at BidnessApp@outlook.com.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes via the app. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.
