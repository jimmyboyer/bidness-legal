Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 11, 2026

Bidness ("we", "our", or "us") is operated by James Boyer, Waterloo, Ontario, Canada. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use the Bidness iOS app.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: name and email address when you create an account.
  • Listing content: photos, titles, descriptions, and prices you submit for auctions.
  • City selection: the city you choose for your listings. We do not collect GPS or precise location data.
  • Purchase records: subscription and in-app purchase receipts, processed by Apple and RevenueCat. We do not store payment card details.
  • Usage data: app activity such as searches, bids, and listing views, collected via Firebase Analytics to improve the app.
  • Communications: messages you send through in-app support or feedback channels.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To operate and provide the Bidness marketplace.
  • To process bids, sales, and in-app purchases.
  • To send transactional notifications (bid updates, auction results, messages).
  • To improve app features and fix bugs.
  • To detect and prevent fraud or policy violations.

3. Third-Party Services

Bidness uses the following third-party services, each governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Firebase (Google): authentication, database, cloud functions, and analytics.
  • RevenueCat: subscription management and purchase validation.
  • Apple: in-app purchases and TestFlight distribution.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with the third-party services listed above as necessary to operate the app, or when required by law.

5. Data Retention

We retain your account and listing data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us at the address below.

6. Children's Privacy

Bidness is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Your Rights (PIPEDA – Canada)

As a Canadian user, you have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal information. To exercise these rights, contact us at support@bidness.app.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes via the app. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.
