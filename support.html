Bidness Support

Local auctions in Waterloo Region and Guelph

Questions, problems, or something that doesn't look right? Email us at BidnessApp@outlook.com and we'll get back to you. Include your display name and the listing title if it's about a specific auction.

Reporting a listing or a person

Open the listing or profile, tap the … menu, and choose Report listing or Report user. For a problem with a deal, open the deal and tap Report a problem with this deal. If you ever feel unsafe, leave and contact the police.

Meeting safely

Every pickup happens at a set meetup spot: a police buy-and-sell exchange zone, a mall food court during mall hours, or a Costco parking lot during store hours. Never at anyone's home. Check the item before you pay, and use the I'm here button so the other person knows you've arrived.

Payments

Buyers pay sellers directly by Interac e-Transfer or cash. Bidness never holds your money. Paying at the pickup, once you've seen the item, is the safest choice. Real e-Transfers arrive in your banking app; don't trust emails that ask you to sign in.

Bidness Pro and boosts

Bidness Pro and listing boosts are bought through Apple. To cancel Pro, open the iPhone Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions. For a refund, visit reportaproblem.apple.com.

Strikes and appeals

You can see your strikes, and appeal one, in the app under Profile → Strikes. Strikes expire 12 months after they're given.

Deleting your account

In the app, go to Profile → Settings → Delete my data. See the Privacy Policy for what's removed and what's kept.
